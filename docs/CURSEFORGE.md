# Coin Forge

**A gold-making companion for World of Warcraft: Forever.** It learns prices from your own auction house scans
and searches, then points out money wherever you are: at the auctioneer, at vendors and out in the world.

## Features

- **Deals at the auction house:** flips, vendor snipes, items vendors sell for less than the AH pays, essence
  conversions, disenchanting, crafts, "make it yourself" and other-AH arbitrage. Best first, with an
  "Only what I can do" filter.
- **Sell tab:** everything in your bags priced to sell. It ignores bait listings and price wars, never
  undercuts your own auctions, and shows the price list so you can check the maths. One click on Post.
- **Crafting:** profit or loss of every recipe your characters know, plus unlearned recipes worth learning.
- **Conversions:** live profit on every essence combine and split. No profession needed.
- **Profitable mobs:** a coin over nameplates of mobs worth farming, learned from your own loot.
- **Vendors:** items worth more on the AH glow green in the merchant window.
- **Tooltips:** AH value, vendor price, disenchant estimate (for enchanters) and the best thing to do.
- **Home screen and minimap button** that explain everything in game.

Coin Forge never buys, posts or clicks for you: every action goes through Blizzard's own UI and your clicks.

## Getting started

1. Open an auction house and press **Scan** on the Coin Forge panel (one full scan every 15 minutes).
2. Scan again on another day: deals need two days of price history.
3. Open each profession window once so your recipes are recorded.
4. Type `/cforge` for the home screen, `/cforge help` for commands.
